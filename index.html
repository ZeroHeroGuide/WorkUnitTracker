📖 Guide

Everything the tracker does, in plain English.

Getting started

  1. Open Tracker. The Hour by hour card is set to the current hour.
  2. Each time you finish a call, tap the matching button. Sales and sign-ups have their own buttons.
  3. Made a mistake? ↶ Undo removes the last tap. Missed an hour? Tap that hour's chip and add the calls there.
  4. At the end of your shift, hit 🏁 Finish & log today.
  5. For a day you didn't tap through (or leave), use Log a business day further down and enter the total.

What each call is worth

ActivityWork units

An over-the-phone dual fuel sign-up is two taps — one electricity, one gas — for 2 WU total. Freshdesk work isn't counted here.

The hourly target

55 WU ÷ 7.5 hours = 7.3 WU an hour

Hit that every hour and the day takes care of itself. Realistic ways to get there:

Short days, Saturdays & leave

  • Every day has an Hours worked value. A shorter day gets a smaller goal: 4 hours = 4 ÷ 7.5 of 55 ≈ 29.3 WU.
  • Saturdays default to 5.5 hours (goal ≈ 40.3). Change defaults in Settings ⚙️.
  • Sick and annual leave are recorded but left out of your average completely.

Your average & catch-up

Your average is hours-weighted: total WU ÷ days worked, where a short day counts as part of a day. Example: 61 WU on a full day plus 30 WU on a 4-hour day is 91 ÷ 1.53 = 59.3 WU/day — on track, not 45.5.

If you're under goal, the Catch-up card shows how many WU your next full day needs to bring your average back to 55, and the simplest way to do it.

The mascot

  • Ahead of goal: laughs and glows red. Behind: cries and glows blue. Both fart. Constantly.
  • Click him for a stop-motion dance — six routines, picked to suit your mood.
  • He reacts to what you enter: every call type, how close the hour is, and how your logged day compares to goal.
  • 🔊 mutes all sounds. 🗣️ makes him read his lines out loud (off by default — your desk neighbours thank you).
  • Hover him and press – to shrink him if he's in the way.

The Stats page

  • Month by month: average per day for your last 12 months, against goal.
  • Month detail: pick any month for its numbers and a calendar of every day.
  • All months: a table of everything you've ever logged. Nothing is deleted.
  • Best times: your strongest weekday and hour of the day.
  • Call mix: how many of each call type you've tapped, and what share of WU came from sales.

Moving your data

Your data lives in your browser. Updates at the same link keep it automatically. To move to a new copy, browser or computer:

  1. In the old version: Stats → Your data → Download CSV.
  2. Open the new version.
  3. Stats → Your data → Upload CSV and pick the file.

Uploading adds the file's days and hours into what's already there — it never wipes anything else. The CSV opens in Excel, and still imports after Excel re-saves it.

FAQ

I tapped calls but my average didn't change

Today's taps count once the day is finished — hit "Finish & log today", or it happens automatically next morning. That way your average doesn't drop at 9am every day.

Can my manager or anyone else see my numbers?

No. Everything is stored in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share the CSV yourself.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your history is wiped from that browser. Upload your latest CSV to get it back — another reason to download one weekly.

I logged a day manually and also tapped calls. Which wins?

A manual entry is kept unless you choose otherwise — "Finish & log today" asks before replacing it.

Can I change the 55 goal or the 7.5-hour day?

Yes — Settings ⚙️. The hourly target, catch-up and every chart update instantly.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts, and the mascot shrinks so he doesn't cover the buttons.

Settings

Every day you log has its own "Hours worked" field — handy if an agent leaves early or works a short Saturday. These two numbers just set the starting default (weekdays pre-fill with the standard hours, Saturdays with the Saturday default); you can always adjust it for that one day. A day's goal is prorated from its hours — e.g. 5.5h vs a 7.5h standard day gives a goal of about 40.3 WU.

One CSV holds every day, every tracked hour and these settings. Upload it into any new version to carry everything over (older .json backups work too).
